# The World Today — Friday, 27 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Friday, 27 November 2026, built for the [make.fun news-image-20261127 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261127).

The hero story is the Kuwait drone strike: two Italian technicians working for Rheinmetall Italia were killed at an air defence station, with Italy and Kuwait blaming Iran and Tehran denying any role. Across seven sections, 45 more stories cover a storm-battered US Thanksgiving (the 100th Macy's parade postponed, 500,000+ homes and businesses without power), the EU's choice of Pablo Hernández de Cos to lead the ECB, a DR Congo Ebola outbreak reaching 12,000 cases, a Bangkok refinery blast, and Jannik Sinner missing the 2027 Australian Open after knee surgery. Every story carries a source grade — CONFIRMED or CLAIM — and a date self-check panel verifies that the date, weekday, issue number and bounty all agree. A timeline and a world map close the page.

## How it works

Running `main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` byte for byte.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
